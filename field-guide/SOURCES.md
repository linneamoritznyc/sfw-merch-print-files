Each claim on the eight pages, the source checked, and the result.

## Changed after the check
- No. 1 shell size: 30 to 100 µm changed to 20 to 100 µm.
- No. 3 hypha width: 2 to 10 µm changed to often 4 to 6 µm; actinobacteria 1 µm changed to 1 to 1.5 µm.
- No. 4 "most numerous" removed (flagellates and amoebae both dominate in bacterial soils); size now from Bodo saltans; "thousands a day" removed.
- No. 5 size range replaced with the USDA ranking and numbers per gram; diet narrowed to mainly bacteria.
- No. 6 size replaced; "most numerous in wet soil" and "some eat other protozoa" removed; division cyst now "usually 4, sometimes 2 or 8".
- No. 7 per gram changed to the USDA per teaspoon figure.
- No. 8 sizes split into mites and springtails.

- No. 2 nematode size: "most are 0.3 to 2 mm" changed to "typically about 1 mm long and 50 µm wide" (USDA Soil Biology Primer, nematodes).
- No. 3 root hair: "about 10 µm or wider" changed to "about 10 to 17 µm" (Wikipedia, Root hair).

Every fact on the pages now has a source. The drawings are SFW's own constructions.
