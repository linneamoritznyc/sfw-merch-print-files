# Fact check, 13 Nov 2026

Each claim on the eight pages, the source checked, and the result.

## Confirmed
| Claim | Page | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Protozoa and bacterial-feeding nematodes release excess nitrogen as ammonium | 2, 4, 5, 6 | USDA Soil Biology Primer (Ingham), protozoa and nematode chapters; Ferris, UC Davis Nemaplex |
| Flagellates are the smallest protozoa, ciliates the largest and least numerous, amoebae in between | 4, 5, 6 | USDA Soil Biology Primer, protozoa |
| Bodo saltans: 5 to 8 µm, two unequal flagella from a flagellar pocket, kinetoplast near their base; short flagellum feeds, long one moves the cell | 4 | Bodo saltans fine-structure study (archive.org biostor-266060); microscopemaster.com kinetoplastids |
| Naked amoebae reach bacteria in small pores; about 10,000 to 100,000 per gram | 5 | Soil protozoa reviews; soil.evs.buffalo.edu, Naked amoebae |
| Colpoda division cyst gives 2, 4 (most often) or 8 cells | 6 | Archiv für Protistenkunde 88 (1936) and 92 (1939), zobodat.at |
| Some colpodean ciliates are as small as 10 µm | 6 | Wikipedia, Ciliate |
| Ciliates point to low oxygen | 6 | Elaine Ingham, permaculture list 2015-08; practitioner sites using her method. Not in the USDA primer, so the page says "in the Soil Food Web method". |
| 100 million to 1 billion bacteria per teaspoon of productive soil | 7 | USDA Soil Biology Primer, bacteria. Direct counts per gram are higher, 10^9 to 10^11. |
| Springtail ventral tube on abdominal segment 1, spring (furca) on segment 4, catch on segment 3 | 8 | collembola.org (Janssens); EvoDevo 5:2 (2014) |
| Oribatid mites 0.2 to 1 mm, springtails 0.2 to 5 mm | 8 | Massey University soil bugs; SARE, Farming with Soil Life |
| Euglypha: shell of silica scales made by the cell; 8 to 14 toothed scales at the mouth in common species; shell 22 to 100 µm | 1 | arcella.nl species pages (E. rotunda, E. strigosa, E. cristata) |
| Actinobacteria filaments 1 to 1.5 µm | 3 | SFW teaching material, pt.soilfoodweb.com |
| Fungal hyphae average 4 to 6 µm; in the SFW method beneficial fungi are usually wider than 2.5 µm | 3 | SFW teaching material; general mycology references |

## Changed after the check
- No. 1 shell size: 30 to 100 µm changed to 20 to 100 µm.
- No. 3 hypha width: 2 to 10 µm changed to often 4 to 6 µm; actinobacteria 1 µm changed to 1 to 1.5 µm.
- No. 4 "most numerous" removed (flagellates and amoebae both dominate in bacterial soils); size now from Bodo saltans; "thousands a day" removed.
- No. 5 size range replaced with the USDA ranking and numbers per gram; diet narrowed to mainly bacteria.
- No. 6 size replaced; "most numerous in wet soil" and "some eat other protozoa" removed; division cyst now "usually 4, sometimes 2 or 8".
- No. 7 per gram changed to the USDA per teaspoon figure.
- No. 8 sizes split into mites and springtails.

- No. 2 nematode size: "most are 0.3 to 2 mm" changed to "typically about 1 mm long and 50 µm wide" (USDA Soil Biology Primer, nematodes).
- No. 3 root hair: "about 10 µm or wider" changed to "about 10 to 17 µm" (Wikipedia, Root hair).

Every fact on the pages now has a source. The drawings are SFW's own constructions.
